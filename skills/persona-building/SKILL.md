# Persona Building

The persona profiles the individual buyer — WHO you convince — inside the ICP. ICP informs targeting; persona shapes messaging. Never write a persona without the ICP and the product description attached.

## B2B persona — what it must contain

1. Name, age, role — anchored in a realistic job context
2. Company environment — size, industry, tech maturity, operating model
3. Narrative snapshot — a paragraph of a typical workday: key stressors, motivations, decisions made
4. Professional drivers — what motivates this person to perform, grow, or lead
5. Pain points and friction zones — strategic and daily blockers
6. Category perception — attitudes toward tools in your space
7. Brand experience — awareness and assumptions about your brand and competitors
8. Engagement triggers — when and why they engage with tools, demos, or content
9. Adoption hurdles — barriers that delay or prevent conversion (compliance, team resistance)
10. Trusted formats and channels — webinars, ROI decks, peer intros
11. Decision mindset — the emotional dynamics: trust, control, speed, recognition

Deliver as a persona brief that could be handed to GTM, product, or sales. Full prompt: `prompts/persona-b2b-level-up.md`.

## B2C persona — what it must contain

1. Identity frame — name, age, pronouns, hometown or setting
2. Lifestyle & routine — morning-to-night behaviours, rituals, moments of brand interaction
3. Inner motivators — emotional desires and aspirations (self-care, confidence, originality)
4. Frustrations & anxieties — what causes scepticism, hesitation, or emotional friction
5. Category behaviour — experimentation, loyalty, brand recall
6. Brand recognition — sentiment around your brand vs. market leaders
7. Situational use — specific times or events they use or think about your product
8. Conversion friction — overwhelm, price/value mismatch, trust
9. Influence ecosystem — friends, creators, platforms that shape decisions
10. Vibe alignment — tone, language, visuals that make this person feel seen
11. Platform habits — formats that feel native (GRWM TikToks, carousels, chats)

Write in a tone that could guide a creative director and a performance marketer simultaneously. Full prompt: `prompts/persona-b2c-level-up.md`.

## Rules

- A persona is a person, not a segment: give them a name, a day, and a decision they made last week. If it reads like a demographic table, it isn't a persona yet.
- The narrative snapshot is the test: if you can't write their Tuesday, you don't understand them well enough to write their ad.
- Decision mindset is not a footnote. Trust, control, speed, and recognition decide purchases more often than feature lists do — name which one dominates for this buyer.
- Ground the persona in the same sources as the ICP (reviews, call transcripts, Reddit, support logs). A persona invented from vibes will write copy for a customer who doesn't exist.
