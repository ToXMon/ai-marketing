# Prompt-Led Marketing

AI is a fast-forward button, not a shortcut. The prompt is the work: a good prompt carries a role, an outcome-driven goal, real context, an explicit format, and real sources — then gets refined over iterations.

## The six best practices

1. **Set the role.** Define who the AI is acting as: GTM strategist, SaaS copywriter, DTC marketing lead, content strategist.
2. **Define the goal.** Outcome-driven: "Generate a content plan for this ICP" — not "write something about our product."
3. **Provide context.** Share personas, ICPs, tone, channels. A prompt without context invents context.
4. **Control the format.** Ask for tables, bullet lists, or specific assets. If you don't specify the shape, you get an essay.
5. **Refine with feedback.** Improve clarity and value over iterations. Never ship draft one.
6. **Stay grounded in sources.** Add market-research results, social threads, customer pain points. Garbage in = garbage out.

## Level 1 vs LEVEL UP

- **Level 1:** "Create an Ideal Customer Profile for a SaaS product that helps remote teams manage their workflows more efficiently." One sentence, no role, no structure, no sources. The output is generic.
- **LEVEL UP:** a role, a numbered list of required outputs, product description and sources attached. The same product returns company type, size, funding stage, pain points, tech stack, trigger events, decision-makers, timeline, channels, priorities, an RTB, and cited evidence.

The gap between the two is the whole skill. If a prompt reads like a Level 1, rewrite it before running it.

## Sources that power prompts

**B2B:** G2 / Capterra reviews (pains and comparisons); LinkedIn comments on influencer posts; sales-call transcripts or Gong snippets; Reddit (r/sales, r/startups); analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester); internal support tickets or Slack threads.

**B2C:** Amazon, Sephora, or App Store reviews; TikTok search or comment threads; influencer videos and community replies; Instagram story polls or Twitter threads; support chat transcripts; Google Trends and YouTube search autofill.

## Rules

- No prompt without a source. If no source exists, say the output is ungrounded and treat it as a hypothesis to validate.
- ICP before persona before content. Never write content without the ICP and persona attached.
- Keep the three prompt types distinct: ICP defines WHO you target, persona defines WHO you convince, content planning defines WHAT you make for them.
- Paste the ICP/persona output into the next prompt — don't re-describe it from memory.
