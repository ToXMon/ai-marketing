# Content Roadmap Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025. Level 1 version: "Create content topics for my SaaS product for remote teams [Insert product description]."*

```
You are a GTM strategist for a B2B SaaS company. Based on product description, ICP and sources below, develop a strategic content roadmap for the next quarter including:

- 3 content pillar themes
- 1 lead magnet idea
- 2 BOFU assets (e.g., comparison, calculator)
- Recommended channels (social, email, events)

Product description: [Insert description]
ICP: [Insert or paste ICP output]
Sources: [Insert if you have any sources]
```

After it runs: every pillar must trace to a persona pain or driver. If the roadmap has three TOFU pillars and no BOFU asset, rebalance before publishing anything.
