# ICP — B2B LEVEL UP Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025. Level 1 version: "Create an Ideal Customer Profile for a SaaS product that helps remote teams manage their workflows more efficiently." Run the LEVEL UP version below.*

```
You are a go-to-market strategist for a B2B SaaS startup. Based on the product description and any provided research sources, generate a comprehensive Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) including:

1. Company Type and Industry
2. Company Size (employees, revenue)
3. Funding Stage and Region
4. Business Model and GTM structure
5. Core Pain Points this product solves
6. Common Tech Stack used by these customers
7. Trigger Events that often lead to a buying decision
8. Decision-Maker Titles and Buying Roles
9. Buying Timeline and Procurement Considerations
10. Where, When, and How these customers typically discover or engage with vendors
11. Top 3 Business Priorities for this type of customer
12. A compelling Reason to Believe (RTB) or value proposition statement that positions the product as uniquely valuable to this ICP
13. If available, integrate supporting context from reliable sources such as:
- G2 reviews
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator data
- Crunchbase company profiles
- Win/loss call notes or customer support transcripts

Product: [INSERT PRODUCT DESCRIPTION HERE]
Sources (if any): [INSERT LINKS OR EXCERPTS]
```

After it runs: check every claim against the sources. Label anything unsourced as an assumption to validate, and name the economic buyer separately from the champion before using the ICP.
