# ICP — B2C LEVEL UP Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025.*

```
You are a consumer brand strategist for a DTC or B2C startup. Based on the product description and any provided customer insight sources, generate a rich Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) including:

1. Demographics (age, gender, location, income range, family/life stage)
2. Psychographics (attitudes, lifestyle traits, behaviors)
3. Life Priorities (e.g., convenience, wellness, fun, savings)
4. Emotional Drivers (e.g., connection, confidence, joy, relief)
5. Shopping Behaviors and Influences (e.g., reviews, social, in-store triggers)
6. Media Consumption Habits (social platforms, content types, devices)
7. Where, When, and How they engage with brands and products
8. Typical Frustrations or Barriers in this product category
9. Key Purchase Drivers (e.g., price, values, quality, trends)
10. Top 3 Goals or Motivations relevant to your product
11. A brand-positioning RTB (Reason to Believe) or value proposition statement that connects emotionally and functionally
12. If available, incorporate data or insights from:
- Amazon, Reddit, or TikTok comments
- Product reviews
- Trend reports (e.g., Pew, Mintel, Exploding Topics)
- Social listening or customer support logs

Product: [INSERT PRODUCT DESCRIPTION HERE]
Sources (if any): [INSERT LINKS OR EXCERPTS]
```

After it runs: check every claim against the sources. Label anything unsourced as an assumption to validate, and split the ICP if it fits more than one distinct buyer.
