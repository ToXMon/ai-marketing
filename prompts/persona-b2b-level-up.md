# Persona — B2B LEVEL UP Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025. Level 1 version: "You are a B2B SaaS content strategist. Create a comprehensive decision-maker persona of a mid-market operations leader."*

```
You are a B2B SaaS content strategist. Based on the product description and provided sources below, generate a comprehensive persona of a mid-market operations leader and include the following:

1. Name, Age, Role – Anchor the profile in a humanized, realistic job context
2. Company Environment – Size, industry, tech maturity, operating model
3. Narrative Snapshot – A paragraph outlining a typical workday (key stressors, motivations, decisions made)
4. Professional Drivers – What motivates this person to perform, grow, or lead?
5. Pain Points and Friction Zones – Strategic and daily blockers affecting their work
6. Category Perception – Attitudes toward tools in your space (e.g., workflow automation, analytics)
7. Brand Experience – Awareness and assumptions about your brand and competitors
8. Engagement Triggers – When and why they engage with tools, demos, or content
9. Adoption Hurdles – Barriers that might delay or prevent conversion (e.g., compliance, team resistance)
10. Trusted Formats and Channels – What formats they prefer (e.g., webinars, ROI decks, peer intros)
11. Decision Mindset – Emotional dynamics behind decision-making: trust, control, speed, recognition

Format: Deliver in a persona brief style that could be handed to GTM, product, or sales.

Product description: [INSERT PRODUCT DESCRIPTION HERE]
Sources: [INSERT LINKS OR EXCERPTS]
```

After it runs: the narrative snapshot is the test — if you can't picture their Tuesday, the persona isn't finished. Attach the ICP this persona sits inside.
