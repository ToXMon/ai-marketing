# Full-Funnel Content Map Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025. Level 1 version: "Create top, middle, and bottom funnel content ideas for my product [Insert product description]."*

```
You are a content strategist at a Series A startup. Using the product description, ICP, persona and sources below, generate a full-funnel content plan that maps 3 core topics across the funnel stages:

- TOFU: Blogs, social, podcast
- MOFU: Case study, comparison, email series
- BOFU: Demo video, calculator, 1-pager

Product description: [Insert description]
ICP: [Insert or paste ICP output]
Persona: [Insert persona]
Sources: [Insert if you have any sources]
```

After it runs: check that each topic appears in all three stages with a different job per stage (reach → evaluate → buy). A topic that only exists at TOFU is an awareness hobby, not a funnel.
