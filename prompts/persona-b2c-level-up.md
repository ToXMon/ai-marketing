# Persona — B2C LEVEL UP Prompt

*Source: Katy Lee, "Prompt-Led Marketing Strategies," NY Tech Week, June 2025. Level 1 version: "You are a DTC marketing lead developing a Gen Z skincare persona for campaign planning."*

```
You are a DTC marketing lead developing a Gen Z skincare persona for campaign planning. Based on the product description and provided sources below, generate a comprehensive persona.

Please include in the persona:

1. Identity Frame – Name, age, pronouns, and hometown or setting
2. Lifestyle & Routine – Morning to night behaviors, rituals, and moments of brand interaction
3. Inner Motivators – Emotional desires and aspirations (e.g., self-care, confidence, originality)
4. Frustrations & Anxieties – What causes skepticism, hesitation, or emotional friction in the category?
5. Category Behavior – Level of experimentation, loyalty, brand recall in skincare and wellness
6. Brand Recognition – Sentiment or narrative around your brand vs. market leaders
7. Situational Use – Specific times or events they use or think about your product
8. Conversion Friction – What could block a purchase (e.g., overwhelm, price/value mismatch, trust)
9. Influence Ecosystem – Friends, creators, platforms that shape their decisions
10. Vibe Alignment – What tone, language, visuals make this person feel seen or included?
11. Platform Habits – Which social or content formats feel native (e.g., GRWM TikToks, carousel posts, chats)

Response style: Write in a tone that could guide a creative director and performance marketer simultaneously.

Product description: [INSERT PRODUCT DESCRIPTION HERE]
Sources: [INSERT LINKS OR EXCERPTS]
```

After it runs: check the influence ecosystem and platform habits against real sources — a persona whose feeds you invented will write copy for a customer who doesn't exist.
